# Code of Conduct

## Purpose

Perfica is an open-source project. Participation should remain professional,
constructive, and safe for contributors and users.

This policy is informed by the principles of Contributor Covenant.

## Expected behavior

Participants should:

- communicate respectfully;
- give technical criticism without attacking people;
- accept constructive review in good faith;
- focus discussion on the project and contribution;
- respect different levels of experience;
- respect privacy;
- acknowledge mistakes and correct them;
- avoid deliberately disruptive behavior.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable conduct includes:

- harassment;
- threats or intimidation;
- discriminatory abuse;
- sexual harassment;
- sustained personal attacks;
- trolling intended primarily to disrupt participation;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- impersonation;
- deliberately abusive or degrading communication.

Technical disagreement is not misconduct when expressed professionally.

## Scope

This policy applies to Perfica-controlled community spaces and to people acting
as official representatives of the project.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may moderate, edit, hide, reject, lock, or remove contributions or
interactions that violate this policy.

Responses may include:

1. correction;
2. warning;
3. temporary participation restriction;
4. permanent participation restriction.

## Reporting conduct problems

Do not publish sensitive conduct reports if doing so would expose private
information or increase harm.

Use a private maintainer communication channel when one is available.

If no private channel is exposed, open only a minimal public request asking for
a private contact path without including sensitive incident details.
